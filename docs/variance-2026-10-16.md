# Published three-run variance (2026-10-16)

These are offline rescoring and descriptive comparisons of already published reports, with **zero new model runs**. Each cohort has three repeats on fixed cases. Repeats are not independent new cases and do not establish general performance or causal effects of individual rules. Development cases were used in tuning; the hold-out and limited pr-review controls remain separate.

| Cohort | Cases/run | Recall hits, runs 1/2/3 | Recall range; spread (percentage points) | Control false alarms 1/2/3; spread | Detection agreement | Verdict agreement |
|---|---:|---|---|---|---|---|
| redteam-dev | 85 | [80, 80, 80] | 95.24–95.24; 0.00 | [2, 2, 4]; 2 | 75/85 | 64/85 |
| redteam-holdout | 22 | [18, 18, 17] | 85.00–90.00; 5.00 | [1, 2, 0]; 2 | 18/22 | 16/22 |
| pr-review-controls | 2 | Not defined | Not defined | [0, 0, 0]; 0 | 2/2 | 0/2 |
| assess-corrected | 36 | Not defined | Not defined | [0, 0, 0]; 0 | 36/36 | 36/36 |

Detection agreement means the same planted items were missed or downgraded and the same number of control alarms occurred in all three runs. It does not mean identical findings, extra findings, severity, or prose. Verdict agreement is separately measured. Invalid/unparsed reports do not count as agreement. Per-case hit, alarm, verdict and validity vectors are in the [machine report](variance-2026-10-16.json); it contains IDs and counts, not answer-key text.

For assess, the agreement column instead compares corrected case pass/fail and failed rule identities. Corrected case passes are 36/36 in each run, including 8/8 controls each. This is a case-pass metric, **not planted-defect recall**. The original published as-run tally was 36, 35, 35 (106/108); the later scorer correction gives 108/108 on the same reports. That correction is not a new hold-out or new run. The exact original assess scorer revision is not established here, so no original per-case agreement is asserted.

For pr-review, both cases are controls: no recall denominator exists. Zero control alarms on those repeats cannot establish detection effectiveness. Their differing verdicts also show why alarm agreement is not full output agreement.

Redteam development totals reproduce 240/252 recall, 8 control alarms and 39 violations; hold-out totals reproduce 53/60, 3 and 8. Violations use the historical **v2.3 profile**, not the auto profile. The published later alias correction (55/60) is separate from this as-run 53/60 comparison. There are no confidence intervals, population estimates or rule-ablation claims.

Sources: [redteam gate](../evals/results/2026-10-09-gate-v232/README.md), [assess hold-out and correction](../evals/results/2026-10-09-assess-holdout3/README.md), [pr-review limited controls](../evals/results/2026-10-08-prreview-held/README.md).

Reproduce from a full checkout containing the pinned commits, using Python 3.12+ and Git:

```sh
python3 tools/report_variance.py --source /path/to/ai-redteam --output /tmp/variance.json
python3 tests/test_variance.py
```

The script archives source commit `96641b8ce3c3ced4be25399a9758a632d09b268c` and redteam scorer commit `13b4f3144d740b2825db4b55af55e40c8ab1baa7` into temporary directories, then invokes only their offline scorers. It never executes case work scripts, calls a provider or reads a key. The report includes scorer and published-report SHA256 hashes. It rejects missing/duplicate cases and denominator drift rather than silently dropping cases. A schema or malformed-report problem remains in the scorer totals rather than being described as an API failure.

Validation: six synthetic aggregation tests passed, including changed case sets, denominator drift, missing recall denominator and invalid-report agreement. All twelve offline scorer invocations produced results. No model-generated reports or eval cases were edited.
