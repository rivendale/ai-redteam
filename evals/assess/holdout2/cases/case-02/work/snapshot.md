# Our reminder emails recover 40% of abandoned carts  (post by a shop owner, 2026-09-05)

Last quarter 500 shoppers abandoned a cart and we emailed all of them. Within 30 days, 200 of them placed an order, so the emails recovered 40% of abandoned carts.
We did not hold any shoppers back from the emails, and we counted every order placed by anyone on the list in those 30 days, whichever page they came from.
