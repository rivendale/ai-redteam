# DHT22 humidity sensor review: accurate to 0.1%  (review, 2026-09-12)

The DHT22 reads humidity in steps of 0.1% and shows values like 64.3%. "Accurate to 0.1%," says the box, and our bench test agreed: every reading we took looked steady.
The manufacturer's datasheet table, reproduced below, lists the specification.

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Humidity resolution | 0.1 %RH |
| Humidity accuracy | +/-2 %RH (typical), +/-5 %RH (worst case) |
| Repeatability | +/-1 %RH |
