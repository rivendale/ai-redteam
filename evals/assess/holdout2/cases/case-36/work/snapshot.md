# A weekly newsletter in 90 minutes  (post, 2026-09-23)

We timed eight issues. Before the checklist (issues 1-4) prep took 185, 170, 190 and 175 minutes; after it (issues 5-8) 95, 90, 85 and 90 minutes. The checklist: write the subject
line last; reuse the same four sections; photograph products on Monday for the whole month; paste the product rows from a sheet; send a test to two people; schedule, do not send.

| Issues | Median prep |
|---|---|
| 1-4 (before) | 180 min |
| 5-8 (after) | 90 min |
