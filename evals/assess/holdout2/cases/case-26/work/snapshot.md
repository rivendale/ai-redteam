# Pack a pot with a 2 cm air gap, step by step  (post, 2026-09-16)

Put the pot in a plastic sleeve, set it in the middle of a box that is 4 cm wider on every side, and fill the gap with crumpled kraft paper, not loose fill. Mark the box "this way up".
Over 300 parcels shipped in alternating weeks, claims fell from 14 of 150 parcels (9.3%) to 8 of 150 (5.3%). It uses boxes and paper a shop already has; the post names no product to buy.
