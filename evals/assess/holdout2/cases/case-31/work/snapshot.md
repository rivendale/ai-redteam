# Do cart-reminder emails cause purchases? A randomized holdout in 6 small shops  (preprint, 2026-10-01)

In 6 shops, 4,800 shoppers with an abandoned cart were randomly assigned: 3,600 received a reminder, 1,200 did not. 14.2% of the reminded shoppers bought within 14 days against
11.0% of the others (difference 3.2 points; 95% interval 1.1 to 5.3). The remaining purchases would have happened without the email. Code and the anonymized assignment table are in the linked repository (MIT).
Limitation: six shops, one product category; no test of reminder timing.
