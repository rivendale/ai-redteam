# DamageBook  --  terms  (read 2026-10-08)

DamageBook is a desktop app (macOS and Windows) for logging damaged parcels: attach a photo, pick a cause (crushed, spilled, wet, broken pot), and see counts by cause and by week. You also enter the week's parcel count, so the one-page weekly report you can print shows claims per 100 parcels, the figure a shop's damage target is set in.
It is free for shops, with no account and no limits. Everything is stored on your computer in a folder you choose, and nothing is sent anywhere.
License: freeware, commercial use allowed. Optional paid support ($120/year) is separate and not needed to use the app.
