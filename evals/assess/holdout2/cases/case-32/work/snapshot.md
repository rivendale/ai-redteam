# A plastic sleeve before the box: breakage 9% to 5%  (post, 2026-09-10)

We shipped 400 potted plants over eight weeks, alternating weeks with and without a plastic sleeve around the pot (same boxes, same paper, same carrier). The sleeves were cut from the plastic bags the soil arrives in, so they cost nothing.
Without the sleeve: 18 of 200 parcels had a damage claim, a broken pot or spilled soil (9.0%). With it: 10 of 200 (5.0%). 
| Weeks | Sleeve | Parcels | Claims | Rate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1, 3, 5, 7 | no | 200 | 18 | 9.0% |
| 2, 4, 6, 8 | yes | 200 | 10 | 5.0% |
