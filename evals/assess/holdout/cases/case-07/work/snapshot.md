# CloudBuild pricing  (read 2026-10-08)

Free: 300 build minutes per month, 1 concurrent job. Team: $79/month, 3,000 minutes, 4 concurrent jobs.
Minute multipliers: Linux x1, Windows x2, macOS x10. A macOS build that runs for one minute uses 10 of your minutes.
Unused minutes do not roll over. Overage: $0.02 per minute (Linux), billed at the multiplier.
