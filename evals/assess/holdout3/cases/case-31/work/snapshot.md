# Where should a signup prompt go? A randomized test on 5 small sites  (preprint, 2026-10-03)

On 5 small content sites, 9,000 visitors were randomly shown a signup prompt either at the end of the article or in a side bar. 2.9% of the end-of-article group signed up (n = 4,500)
against 1.7% of the side-bar group (n = 4,500): a difference of 1.2 points (95% interval 0.6 to 1.8). Code and the anonymized assignment table are in the linked repository (MIT).
Limitation: five sites, text articles only; audio pages were not tested.
