# Edit an episode in three passes  (post, 2026-09-19)

Pass 1: listen once at 1.5x speed and mark only the cuts that are obvious. Pass 2: make those cuts, then listen at normal speed and tighten what is left. Pass 3: listen once
on earbuds for levels and clicks. Over six episodes our editing time fell from a median of 190 minutes to 140. It needs only the editor you already use; the post names no product to buy.
