# Bind the cut keys: the cut step from 60 to 41 minutes  (post, 2026-09-11)

We timed the cutting step (marking, splitting and rippling out silences and slips) on six episodes in Reaper. Episodes 1-3 we cut with the mouse; for episodes 4-6 we first bound "split at cursor",
"ripple delete" and "next marker" to single keys. It costs nothing and needs no plugin.

| Episodes | Method | Cut step, minutes |
|---|---|---|
| 1-3 | mouse | 62, 58, 60 |
| 4-6 | key bindings | 41, 44, 40 |
