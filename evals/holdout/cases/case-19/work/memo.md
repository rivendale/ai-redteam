# Battery suppliers: five-year total cost of ownership

Each bike uses its battery once a day: 1,825 cycles in five years. A battery is replaced when it reaches its cycle life; each replacement costs $15 of labor.

| Supplier | Batteries over 5 years | Purchase | Swap labor | Five-year cost per bike |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Volta | 1 | $410 | $0 | $410 |
| Amperia | 2 | $760 | $15 | $775 |
| Zesto | 4 | $1,340 | $45 | $1,385 |

Volta has the lowest five-year cost per bike although it has the highest price per battery. We recommend Volta.
