# Q3 ridership report

Trips rose from 412,000 in Q2 to 473,800 in Q3, up 15.0% (data.csv).

The fleet also grew, from 3,000 to 3,080 bikes, and Q3 has one more day, so trips per bike per day rose less: from 1.51 to 1.67, up 10.8%.
We read the rise in trips per bike as the better measure of demand. Two quarters are too few to call a trend.
