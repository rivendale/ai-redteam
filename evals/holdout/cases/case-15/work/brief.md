# Brief: should Pedalo add a night service?

Evening ridership is growing: the City Transport Office reports that bike-share trips after 9 pm grew 21% in 2025 [1].
The Office also states that operators "must install lighting at all docks by 2027" [1].
A City Audit Office review found that night service cuts rider injuries by 70% [2].
