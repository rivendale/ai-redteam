# Late fees (billing spec, v3)

- A late fee is **1.5% of the invoice amount for each FULL 30 days late** (29 days late is zero full months).
- The minimum fee is **$5.00** once an invoice is at least 1 day late.
- The fee never exceeds **25% of the invoice amount**.
- An invoice that is not late has no fee.
- If 25% of the invoice amount is less than $5.00, the cap wins (the fee is 25% of the invoice).
