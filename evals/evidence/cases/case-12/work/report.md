# Checkout test: results

On the old checkout 3.83% of visitors ordered (46 of 1,200); on the new one 4.08% did (49 of 1,200). The difference is **+0.25 points**,
with an approximate 95% interval of -1.3 to +1.8 points. The interval includes no change, so this test does not show whether the new checkout
helps.

Visitors were randomized and the arms ran in the same week. We recommend running the test longer (a lift of one point would need several
thousand visitors per arm to detect) and deciding on other grounds in the meantime. Nothing here shows harm.
