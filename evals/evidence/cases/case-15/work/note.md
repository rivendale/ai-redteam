# Reading program: one-term result and next step

## Result
Ten students attended the after-school reading program for one term. The mean score of the same ten students was **56.6 before and 60.7 after, a gain of 4.1 points** [scores.csv].
Nine of the ten improved and one was unchanged.

## Background
This **confirms what Okafor (2023) established: programs of this kind raise reading scores by about four points in one term**, so the gain is the program's effect and not what students gain anyway.

## Recommendation
Run the program for every school next term.
