# Retention report: the new onboarding

Prepared for the product review.

## Result
30-day retention for all new users was **46.7%** in April to June on v1 (140 of 300) and **52.0%** in July on v2 (104 of 200):
a difference of **+5.3 points**. An approximate 95% interval for the difference is -3.6 to +14.3 points, so one month of v2 does not
separate a real gain from noise. This is a before/after comparison (April to June against July), not a randomized test, so season and user mix are not
separated from the flow. Data cutoff 2 September 2026, so every July sign-up has had its 30 days.

Among v2 users who finished setup, retention was 57.9% (81 of 140); among those who did not it was 38.3% (23 of 60). Finishers are a self-selected
subgroup and v1 has no comparable measure, so neither figure is compared with the baseline.

## Method
Retention is the share of users still active on day 30, over everyone who signed up in the month. Counts are in signups.csv.

## Recommendation
Keep v2 (the interval does not rule out a small decline, down to -3.6 points, but nothing here shows one) and re-measure after September before
claiming an improvement.
