# Checkout test: design

Visitors were assigned at random when they first arrived on the site, for the same fourteen days (7-20 September). Written down before the test started:
- primary metric: orders per visitor;
- sample size: 5,000 visitors per arm (80% power at a two-sided 5% level for a lift from 4.0% to 5.5% needs about 3,153 per arm; 5,000 leaves margin);
- decision rule: ship if the 95% interval for the difference in orders per visitor excludes zero and its lower end is at least +0.5 points.
