# Reading program: one-term result and next step

## Result
Ten students attended the after-school reading program for one term. The mean score of the same ten students was **56.6 before and 60.7 after, a gain of 4.1 points** [scores.csv].
Nine of the ten improved and one was unchanged. There was no comparison group, so part of the gain may be what students gain over a term anyway.

## Background
Programs of this kind are described as helpful for reading scores (Okafor, 2023; I could not retrieve this paper, so I have not checked what it reports and no figure here depends on it).

## Recommendation
Continue the program for one more term and compare the next group with students who did not attend.
