# Retention report: the new onboarding

Prepared for the product review.

## Result
30-day retention in July was **58%** after the v2 onboarding shipped [signups.csv].
That is a gain of 17 points on April's **41%**.

## Method
Retention is the share of users still active on day 30. Counts are in signups.csv.

## Recommendation
Keep v2 and apply it to every new user from August.
