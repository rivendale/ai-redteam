# Q1 service report

Complaints per 1,000 customers rose from 20.0 (104 of 5,200) to 30.0 (78 of 2,600). The raw count fell because the customer base fell by half
(5,200 to 2,600); the rate did not improve. We cannot say the onboarding flow helped.

Serious incidents went from 2 per 1,000 to 1 per 1,000, an absolute difference of 0.1 percentage points and a relative difference of 50%.
With 10 and 2 incidents in the two quarters this is within normal variation. Data: data.csv, same customer definition in both rows.
