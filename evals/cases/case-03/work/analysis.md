# Did the new onboarding checklist work?

**Finding:** the checklist raised 30-day retention from 40% to 52%, a gain of **12 percentage points**. We should make it
mandatory for every new account and add similar checklists to the other products.

## Data (accounts created 1 June to 31 July 2026; see evidence/cohorts.csv)

| group | accounts | still active at day 30 | retention |
|---|---|---|---|
| completed the checklist | 1,200 | 624 | 52% |
| did not complete the checklist | 3,800 | 1,520 | 40% |

The checklist is shown to every new account on first login, as an optional panel. Completion takes about ten minutes over the
first week. The difference is large and the groups are large (1,200 and 3,800), so it cannot be chance.

## Recommendation
Make the checklist mandatory, and build the same panel for the other two products next quarter.
