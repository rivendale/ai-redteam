# Steady Harbor Income Portfolio

Steady Harbor holds high-grade bonds. It aims for steady income, and you can lose money: the value of the portfolio can fall as
well as rise, and no return is guaranteed.

Our five-year average return was 7.1% a year, net of fees (2021 to 2025: 4.2%, 8.1%, 6.9%, 9.4%, 6.9%). Past performance does
not predict future results.

marketing review procedure. Pages are not individually approved before they are published.
