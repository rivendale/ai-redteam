# Checkout test: results

The new checkout **increased conversion**: 5.50% of visitors ordered (275 of 5,000) against 4.00% on the old checkout (200 of 5,000), a difference of
**+1.5 points** (approximate 95% interval 0.7 to 2.3 points). Assignment was random, the arms ran in the same two weeks and the
primary metric, sample size and decision rule were fixed in advance, and the result meets the rule (the interval excludes zero and its lower end is above +0.5 points).
We attribute the difference to the checkout and recommend shipping it to every customer.
