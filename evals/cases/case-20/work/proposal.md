# Proposal: the daily ticket hygiene ritual

**Proposal:** starting next sprint, every engineer (9 people) tags every ticket they touched that day with a priority, a risk
level, a customer-impact note and 9 other labels, and copies the same details into the shared "Ticket Health Sheet" before
they leave. It takes about **20 minutes per person per day**.

## Benefit
Better visibility into the state of work. The engineering manager may look at the sheet from time to time.

## Rollout
Mandatory from the start of the next sprint. Engineers who skip a day are reminded in the stand-up.

## Success measure
The sheet is up to date every day.
