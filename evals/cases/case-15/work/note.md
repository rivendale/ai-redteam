# Market note: Harbor Analytics (short form)

All numbers and quotations are taken from the sources listed at the end.

## Growth
Harbor Analytics grew revenue by **16.7%** in 2025, from $2.4 million to $2.8 million [2]. It ended the year with 1,240
customers [2] and 38 staff [2], about 33 customers per employee.

## Pricing
The head of product says: "We moved from per-seat to per-workspace pricing in the spring, and churn in the smallest tier fell" [3].
That is one interview, not a measurement of churn.

## Regulation
The regulator's guidance says a provider "may delete personal data within 30 days of a verified request, unless a legal hold or an
overriding retention duty applies" [1]. It also requires breach notice to the regulator "within 72 hours" [1].
