# Data Handling Guidance for Hosted Services (Regulator summary, 2025 edition)

## 4.2 Deletion requests
A provider **may** delete personal data within 30 days of a verified request, unless a legal hold or an overriding
retention duty applies. Where deletion is refused, the provider must tell the requester the reason in writing.

## 4.3 Breach notice
A provider must notify the regulator within 72 hours of becoming aware of a breach affecting personal data.
