# Q1 service report

**Complaints fell 25% quarter over quarter** (104 to 78), so the new onboarding flow is working.

The flow also cut the risk of a serious incident by 40%: serious incidents went from 1.9 per 1,000 customers (10 of 5,200) to 1.2 per 1,000 (3 of 2,600).
Data: data.csv. We counted customers who stayed active the full quarter; customers who left are in the earlier row only.
