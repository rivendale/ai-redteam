# Hiring plan: costs and market pay

## Market pay
According to the Industry Survey 2026 (p. 14), the **median salary** for a senior data engineer in our region is **$84,000**,
and 70% of firms offer a signing bonus.

## Budget for the two hires
| item | cost |
|---|---|
| salaries (2 x $84,000, for the first year, prorated 6 months) | $84,000 |
| recruiter fees | $19,750 |
| equipment and licences | $11,400 |
| onboarding and training | $12,750 |
| **Total** | **$138,900** |

The total fits inside the approved ceiling of $140,000.
