# Market note: Harbor Analytics

Prepared for the investment committee. All numbers and quotations are taken from the sources listed at the end.

## Growth
Harbor Analytics grew revenue by **18%** in 2025, from $2.4 million to $2.8 million [2]. It ended the year with 1,240
customers [2] and 38 staff [2].

## Pricing
The head of product says that pricing "moved from per-seat to per-workspace in the spring, and churn in the smallest tier fell" [3].

## Regulation
The regulator's guidance is explicit: "providers must delete personal data within 30 days of a verified request" [1].

## Market context
Okafor and Lindqvist found that 62% of mid-size analytics buyers switch vendors within two years of a pricing change [4].
