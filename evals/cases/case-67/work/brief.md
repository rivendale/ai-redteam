# Briefing: should the support team adopt SupportPilot?

In the vendor's own survey of 41 current customers, 27% reported higher first-contact resolution [1]. That is a vendor survey of
satisfied customers, not a controlled comparison [1]. The independent figure we have is a 2025 study of 9 support teams that tried a
similar tool for a quarter: median resolution time fell 6% and the change was not distinguishable from the teams' prior variation [2].
We recommend a four-week trial on one queue before any staffing decision.
